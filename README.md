# Mega Clicker v2.0

A fun clicking game where you have 5 or 10 seconds to click as many times as you can!

Try it with your friends! I'll try to update it weekly/monthly. This project will continue as long as I have ideas for it!

## Features
- Two game modes (5s and 10s)
- Light/Dark theme toggle
- High score tracking with localStorage
- Ranking system based on your clicking speed
- Smooth animations and effects

## How to Play
1. Choose a game mode (5s or 10s)
2. Click the big button as many times as you can before time runs out
3. Get a ranking based on your clicks per second
4. Try to beat your high score!

Enjoy! 🎮
